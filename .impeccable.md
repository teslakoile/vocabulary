## Design Context

### Users
One person, Kyle, drilling his own professional word list. Mostly on a phone as
an installed PWA, opened from a 1pm nudge for a few cards at a time, and
sometimes on a laptop with the number keys. The job is to answer a card, read
the caution, and move on. Capture competes with typing a line into Google Keep.

### Brand Personality
Calm, bookish, light. The reference is cora.computer: a clear blue sky, white
book-serif headlines set straight onto it, white cards with dark text floating
on the blue, and soft buttons. It should feel like looking up from a desk,
never like a dashboard.

### Aesthetic Direction
- One theme. The page is sky blue `--sky` (#3a7bc4), a little lighter toward
  the bottom. All text on it is white; quiet text is white at 84%.
- Two families, as on the reference. EB Garamond (standing in for Signifier) is
  for headlines only: the word, the prompt, a sense title, the app name. Host
  Grotesk (standing in for Switzer) is for everything else. No italics and no
  letter-spacing anywhere.
- Seven sizes and no others, defined as `--text-*` tokens in `src/index.css`:
  `caption` 12, `small` 14, `body` 17, `lead` 22, `title` 28 (serif),
  `display-sm` 36 (serif), `display` 55 (serif). Line height rides with the
  size; only single-line chips override it, with `leading-none`.
- Three sans weights: 400 on a card, 500 on the sky and on buttons, 600 for a
  label or the word inside a card. The serif is always 400.
- Spacing is on a 4pt grid with no half steps. Within a group the gap is 8;
  between groups it is 24; between cards in a list it is 12; inside a card the
  padding is 16.
- Anything you tap or type into is a white card with the `lift` shadow.
  Buttons are white for the main action and a glass outline for the rest.
- Components, one shape each:
  - Fields: `.field` in `src/index.css`. A white card on the sky, a pale blue
    tint inside a card, white with a blue ring when focused.
  - Buttons: the primary is white on the sky and blue on a card; when it
    is disabled it keeps its shape and turns to glass or pale blue rather than
    fading. Secondary actions are ghost buttons or the glass outline.
  - `Notice`: every warning and offer (failed save, nudge, install hint). Icon
    in a round chip, text, optional actions. Red chip for errors, blue for
    offers.
  - `Caution`: white card with a red "Careful" header on the sky; a flat tinted
    panel inside a card.
  - Two small corners. 12px for anything that floats on the sky: cards,
    answer tiles, fields, the main 56px button, the Careful card, notices.
    8px for anything small or nested in a card: buttons, chips, badges, the
    verdict tag, and the fields, panels and buttons inside a card. Circles are
    only for icons, avatars and dots. Kyle prefers corners on the square side,
    so do not go rounder. Anything with `lift` loses its shadow inside a card,
    so nothing reads as a card in a card.
  - Press feedback is `scale(0.96)` everywhere; `<Button static>` turns it off.
    Small buttons are 36 to look at and 40 to hit.
- `.paper` flips the tokens on a card: text goes dark, muted goes grey, weight
  drops to 400, and the primary button goes blue.
- Red appears only on a card: the "Careful" label and a wrong verdict.
- The one decoration is `Sky`, a painting of cloud and a tree at the foot of a
  question and the key screen. It sits in the flow so white cards never land on
  white cloud. `scripts/art/sky.md` records how it was made.
- Anti-references: the earlier green canopy and cream paper themes, dark
  dashboards, progress badges, side-stripe callouts.

### Design Principles
1. The word is the hero. On any screen that shows one, it is the largest thing.
2. White on blue for reading, dark on white for touching.
3. One shadow, one radius, one blue.
4. Nothing counts up. No streaks, totals, or completion states.
5. Left-aligned throughout. Only the top bar and the main button centre
   anything.
